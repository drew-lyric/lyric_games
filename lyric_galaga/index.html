FLIGHT MANUAL / LYRIC GAMES

Protect the culture.

On your phone, play sideways. Enable tilt to steer, or drag the joystick. Tap or hold the arena to fire. Use the Weapon button to cycle antibodies and Activate to call macrophages. In white mode, hold to charge and release to launch an opsonin. Open Pause to change controls or recalibrate tilt.

Clear five sectors, then defeat a boss whose vulnerable core switches between four antigen colors. You have three lives. After a hit, your ship is protected for two seconds.

01 / MATCH
Move with ← → ↑ ↓. Vertical travel covers a band about 20% of the arena height near the bottom. Hold SPACE to fire. Use Z to cycle antibodies. Only the matching color binds and destroys an antigen. New colors unlock in sectors 2, 3, and 4.
02 / PLACE
Shoot rare white orbs with any antibody to collect opsonins. Use Z to cycle to white. Hold SPACE to charge the power bar, then release: more power places the orb farther up the arena. Flying orbs pass through antigens.
03 / ENGULF
Press X to activate all settled opsonins. After half a second, macrophages engulf nearby antigens of every color. Their attack also damages a nearby boss. Inventory carries between sectors; placed orbs clear at each transition.
04 / IVIG
Hit the rare rainbow antibody as it crosses the arena. For 30 seconds of active play, your ship glows and fires eight antibodies per volley: two of each color. White mode still places opsonins.
05 / RESPONSE
Toggle Antigen response in the sidebar. When off, antigens and the boss stop firing, and waves and summons arrive 50% faster. Their movement and collision damage stay the same.

P or ESC pauses. Switching windows pauses automatically. Switching weapons or pausing cancels a charge without using an opsonin.
