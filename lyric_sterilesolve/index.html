Controls
Prepare: Drag equipment from the supply rack into STAGING. Drop a flask on a media vat to choose a fill amount. Click the white ethanol bottle to spray every object in staging.
Assemble: Move clean equipment into INCUBATOR. Drag sterile tubing ends onto ports. Drag a sterile blue clamp onto tubing; click an installed clamp to open or close it. Click a selector valve to switch outlets.
Run: Set pump rates in the inspector, then GO. Rates lock; everything else stays interactive. There is no manual pause.
Reactors: Total flow and blend must match simultaneously. The listed mL is processed throughput. Outlets carry the same blend, splitting equally unless downstream pumps force a different split. Media may pass through two reactors; then it is spent.
Recover: Flow faults have two seconds to fix. Dirty equipment in the incubator ends the attempt immediately. Retry restores setup; Reset restores level entry. Ethanol refreshes every three successful levels.
